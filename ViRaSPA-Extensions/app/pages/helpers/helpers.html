Helpers

Small optional APIs, each in its own namespace and loaded at startup (startup.js helpers) or with a component that needs it.

forms and data-entry

Required fields get their * by themselves. Enter moves to the next field and Shift+Enter goes back. Enter on the last field submits, and the validation messages explain any problem. "Read-only" switches the whole form and restores it exactly.

filter

Letter case and accents are ignored, and every word must appear: "elodie aru" finds Élodie in Arusha. Matches are highlighted.
