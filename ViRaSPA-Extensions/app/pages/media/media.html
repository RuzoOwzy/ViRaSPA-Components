Media viewer and image cropper

Both are written entirely in our own code. vs-media-viewer shows images, SVG, PDF, video and documents full screen, with zoom, pan, rotation and a gallery. vs-image-cropper crops a picture to a fixed or free shape.

A gallery

Click a picture. Zoom with the wheel, a pinch or + and −, pan by dragging or with the arrows when zoomed, rotate with R, fit with 0. Move between items with Left/Right or a swipe, and close with Escape.

A document (opens as a link)

Crop a picture

Drag the box or its handles. With the keyboard, the arrows move it (Shift: 10 pixels), Alt+arrows resize it, and + − R zoom and rotate. You can also choose or drop your own image: nothing is uploaded.
