vs-pagination

First, previous, next and last, a page box and the page size. It is used inside vs-table, and works with any list of your own.

Page 1, 20 per page.

<vs-pagination total="135" page-size="20" page-sizes="10,20,50" /> pager.addEventListener("vs-page-change", (e) => load(e.detail.page, e.detail.pageSize));
