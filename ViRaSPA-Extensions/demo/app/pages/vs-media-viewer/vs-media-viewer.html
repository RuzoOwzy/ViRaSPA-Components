vs-media-viewer

Click a picture. Zoom with the wheel, a pinch or + and −, pan by dragging or with the arrows when zoomed, rotate with R, fit with 0. Move between items with Left/Right or a swipe, and close with Escape. It shows images, SVG, PDF, video and documents, all in our own code.
