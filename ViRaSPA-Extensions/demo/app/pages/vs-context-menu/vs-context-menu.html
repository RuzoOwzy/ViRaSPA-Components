vs-context-menu

A right-click menu. It also opens with a long press on a phone and with Shift+F10 or the Menu key, and works fully with the keyboard.

Right-click here (or focus it and press Shift+F10, or long-press on a phone).

<vs-context-menu for="#orders"> <button value="open" data-icon="lucide:external-link">Open</button> … </vs-context-menu> menu.addEventListener("vs-menu-select", (e) => run(e.detail.value));
