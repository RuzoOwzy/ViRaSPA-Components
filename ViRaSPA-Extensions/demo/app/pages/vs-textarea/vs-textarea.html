vs-textarea

It grows as you type, always keeping an empty line ready below the text, up to max-rows; then it scrolls.

Overlap: grows over what is below

With overlap it rests at rest-rows (here one line). While you type it opens over the content below instead of pushing it down, and it goes back to one line when you leave it, however long the text.

In a table row

The comments stay one line high in their rows. The one you are typing in opens over the rows below it, even inside the table's scrolling area.

<vs-textarea name="note" rows="2" max-rows="6" maxlength="200" show-count /> <vs-textarea name="comment" overlap rest-rows="1" max-rows="8" /> <column field="comment" label="Comment" editor="textarea"></column> (vs-table-entry)
